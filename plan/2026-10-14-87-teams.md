# Plan: Microsoft Teams outgoing webhooks trigger rules

## Approved decisions

- **`signature: teams-hmac`:**
  - the secret is the base64 security token Teams shows;
  - when the value is known, it must decode as standard base64 to at least
    16 bytes, skipping `config.StubSecret`;
  - `signature_header`, `timestamp_header`, `token_header`, `token_field`
    and `id` are refused.
- **`reply_text`** is also allowed with `teams-hmac`, up to 500 characters.
- **The handler, `teams-hmac`:**
  - **header:** `Authorization: HMAC <base64 32 bytes>`;
  - **key:** the base64-decoded secret. A bad key gets 500 "secret
    misconfigured", never echoed;
  - **check:** `hmac.Equal` over HMAC-SHA256(key, body). A failure gets
    401 "signature invalid";
  - **header dropped** from the event.
- **After verification:**
  - **the replay key** is the activity `id` (a string), else the body hash;
  - **the response** is always 200 `application/json`
    `{"type":"message","text": reply_text or ""}`, a duplicate included.
- **The catalog's `teams`:** a required `token` and an optional `reply`,
  creating `<name>-hooks` (`hook: true`) with the Workflows setup text.
- **The `teams-ask-agent` template:** an agent with no tools, then an
  Adaptive Card post through a Workflows incoming webhook, using the `%q`
  shortcut and a `sorry` step.
- **A full Teams bot is out of scope.**
